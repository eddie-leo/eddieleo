About Me

A little more about who I am

Your photo

Where I'm from

I grew up in Vancouver, surrounded by mountains and rain — which probably explains my love for cozy interfaces and warm color palettes. I moved to the Bay Area eight years ago and have been designing products here ever since, working with startups and established companies alike.

My experience

Over the past eight years I've led design across fintech, healthtech, and B2B SaaS — from early-stage MVPs to products used by millions. I've worn many hats: individual contributor, design lead, and cross-functional partner to engineering and product. I thrive in environments where design has a seat at the strategy table.

Values I design by

  • Clarity over cleverness — interfaces should explain themselves.
  • Accessibility is non-negotiable — good design works for everyone.
  • Iterate with evidence — opinions are fine; data and user feedback are better.
  • Collaboration beats heroics — the best products are built together.

Strengths

  • Translating complex problems into simple, shippable solutions
  • Facilitating workshops and aligning stakeholders around a shared vision
  • Building and maintaining design systems that scale with teams
  • Rapid prototyping and high-fidelity visual craft
  • Mentoring junior designers and raising the bar for design quality
